[FE Stokes Re-Pair](https://fe-nerd-games.github.io/FEStokesRePair/) is a nerdy card game about finite elements.
In the game, Stokes discretizations are composed from the cards.
Whether a discretization is indeed stable, optimally convergent and/or pressure robust can be checked in the [cheat sheet / look-up table](https://github.com/fe-nerd-games/FEStokesRePair/raw/refs/heads/main/table.pdf).
