I am a postdoctoral researcher at CERMICS, École nationale des ponts et chaussées, and at Inria Paris in the team SERENA, where I work with Alexandre Ern on unfitted finite element methods for the wave equation.

Before that, I was a postdoc at the University of Göttingen, working with Christoph Lehrenfeld on unfitted finite elements for Maxwell's equations.
I did my PhD at the Institute of Applied Mathematics of Leibniz University Hannover under the supervision of Sven Beuchler (2023, _summa cum laude_), with a thesis on applications of special functions in high order finite element methods.
Before that, I did my B.Sc. and M.Sc. at the University of Bremen.

My research interests include

- unfitted and cut finite element methods,
- high order finite elements, their basis functions and fast assembly,
- special functions, orthogonal polynomials and symbolic computation,
- time-dependent problems such as the wave and Maxwell's equations.
